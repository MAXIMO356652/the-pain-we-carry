AUTHOR BIOGRAPHY

Maximo — The Waiting-Maximo

Maximo is a Zambian writer, learner and creative thinker whose work explores pressure, responsibility, uncertainty, disappointment, ambition, faith, endurance and hope.

Writing under the name The Waiting-Maximo, he gives words to experiences that many people carry quietly. His writing is reflective, direct and grounded in real life, with a focus on growth and the courage to continue when the future is not yet clear.

“We may not choose every chapter life gives us, but we can choose how we write the next one.”

About The Waiting-Maximo

The name represents patience while becoming, endurance while building, and faith while the future is still unknown.

Waiting is not surrender. Sometimes waiting means preparing, learning, growing and becoming ready for what comes next.

Why I Write

I write about things people often carry silently — pressure, loneliness, sacrifice, disappointment, ambition, failure, faith and hope.

A smile does not always mean that everything is fine. Sometimes a person simply chooses to keep moving.

To the Reader

If you found this book because you are carrying something nobody else understands, I hope these pages give you a place to breathe.

You are allowed to feel the pain. Just don't let the pain write your whole story.

Still waiting. Still learning. Still becoming. Still writing.
