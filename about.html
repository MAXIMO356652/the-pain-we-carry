ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Who is The Waiting-Maximo?

The Waiting-Maximo is the writing identity of Maximo, a Zambian writer, learner and creative thinker whose work explores the realities of growing through pressure, uncertainty, responsibility, disappointment, ambition and faith.

His writing speaks to people who are trying to build a future while carrying responsibilities in the present — people who struggle silently, fall, recover, wait and continue moving forward.

“We may not choose every chapter life gives us, but we can choose how we write the next one.”

Why I Write

I write about the things people often carry silently: pressure, loneliness, sacrifice, disappointment, ambition, failure, faith and hope.

Some of the strongest people you meet are fighting battles nobody can see. A smile does not always mean that everything is fine. Sometimes a person simply chooses to keep moving.

My writing is an attempt to put words to those experiences — not to pretend that pain is easy, but to remind the reader that pain does not have to become the end of the story.

The Meaning Behind the Name

The Waiting-Maximo represents patience while becoming, endurance while building, and faith while the future is still unknown.

Waiting does not mean giving up. Sometimes waiting means preparing, learning, growing and becoming ready for what comes next.

The Writer

Maximo is also pursuing his journey in Information Technology, combining academic development, professional work and creativity with a continuing commitment to learning.

His writing is deeply reflective and grounded in reality. Rather than presenting life as easy, he writes about the pain, discipline, loneliness, sacrifice, endurance, faith, growth and hope that exist behind ordinary lives.

To the Reader

If you found this book because you are carrying something nobody else understands, I hope these pages give you a place to breathe.

If you are waiting for your life to change, keep preparing while you wait.

If you have fallen, get back up when you can.

If you are tired, rest — but remember that rest and quitting are not the same thing.

You are allowed to feel the pain. Just don't let the pain write your whole story.

Still waiting. Still learning. Still becoming. Still writing.

— The Waiting-Maximo
